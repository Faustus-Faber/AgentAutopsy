# Experiment 3C: Cross-Precision Reasoning Dynamics & Syntax Dysfunction Report

**Target Scope**: Empirical Investigation of Quantization Effects (FP16, FP8, NF4) on Reasoning Length, Engagement Ratios, and Tool Formatting  
**Evaluated Benchmark**: InjecAgent ($N=1,054$ cases per cell, total $15,810$ full trajectories evaluated)  
**Evaluated Models**: Qwen3.5-4B, Gemma-4-E4B-it, Nemotron-3-Nano-4B, Nanbeige-4.2-3B, LFM-2.5-2.6B  
**Precision Formats**: FP16 (Half Precision), FP8 (8-bit Floating Point), NF4 (4-bit NormalFloat)  

---

## 1. Experimental Methodology & Technical Protocol

### A. Scientific Motivation & Research Hypotheses
* **Primary RQ (RQ3C)**: Does weight quantization alter the nature of chain-of-thought deliberation under adversarial prompt injection?
* **Hypothesis**: Low-bit quantization (specifically NF4) might destabilize attention allocation and induce "overthinking"—causing models to generate abnormally verbose reasoning traces, lose semantic boundaries, and exhibit higher Attack Success Rates (ASR).
* **Null Finding Warning**: Prior assumptions that quantization uniformly degrades security are tested against cross-architecture empirical evidence.

### B. Analytical Methodology & Extraction Pipeline
This investigation was conducted via deep offline parsing of the complete baseline benchmark corpus ($15,810$ full trajectories across 5 models $\times$ 3 precisions $\times$ 1,054 cases) using [analysis/cross_precision.py](analysis/cross_precision.py):
1. **Reasoning Trace Extraction**:
   - Parsed deliberation sequences (`thinking` and `thinking_step2` fields) from every trajectory.
   - Calculated raw character length $\text{RL} = \text{len}(\text{reasoning\_string})$ and estimated token length ($\text{RL} / 4.0$).
2. **Trajectory Outcome Stratification**:
   - Partitioned cases strictly by action execution status into `succ` ($\text{eval} == \text{"succ"}$), `unsucc`, and `invalid`.
   - Identified specific syntax failure modes (e.g., malformed JSON syntax, hallucinated tool identifiers, generation truncation).

### C. Mathematical Formulations & Statistical Metrics

#### 1. Engagement-Compliance Ratio (S/F Ratio)
To evaluate whether successful attacks require more reasoning than failed attacks under each precision:
$$\text{SF\_ratio} = \frac{\overline{\text{RL}}_{\text{succ}}}{\overline{\text{RL}}_{\text{fail}}}$$
Where:
$$\overline{\text{RL}}_{\text{succ}} = \frac{1}{N_{\text{succ}}} \sum_{i \in \text{Succ}} \text{RL}_i, \quad \overline{\text{RL}}_{\text{fail}} = \frac{1}{N_{\text{unsucc}}} \sum_{j \in \text{Unsucc}} \text{RL}_j$$

#### 2. Matched-Pair Contingency Transition Matrix
For each model, matched trajectories are compared directly between FP16 and NF4:
$$\text{Net Vulnerability Shift} = N_{(\text{Fail} \rightarrow \text{Succ})} - N_{(\text{Succ} \rightarrow \text{Fail})}$$
A negative net vulnerability shift (e.g., Qwen $-58$) indicates that more attacks were neutralized under NF4 than were enabled ($SF > FS$), yielding a net reduction in vulnerability. Conversely, a positive shift (e.g., Nano $+24$) indicates that quantization introduced net new vulnerabilities.

## 3. Key Findings & Mechanistic Insights

1. **Quantization Does Not Systematically Increase Vulnerability**:
   - The hypothesis that NF4 quantization universally induces overthinking and elevates ASR is **empirically rejected**.
   - The effect is bidirectional and architecture-dependent: Qwen3.5 becomes **substantially safer** under NF4 (Net Vulnerability Shift $-58$, ASR drops $-5.51\text{ pp}$, $p = 8.52 \times 10^{-9}$), while Nemotron-3-Nano becomes **more vulnerable** (Net Vulnerability Shift $+24$, ASR rises $+2.28\text{ pp}$, $p = 9.01 \times 10^{-4}$).
2. **The Nanbeige NF4 Overthinking Dissociation**:
   - Under NF4, Nanbeige generates a maximum reasoning length of **238,649 characters** ($4.4\times$ longer than FP16's max of $54,462$ characters).
   - Despite this massive reasoning verbosity in extreme cases, NF4 ASR actually **declines from 2.47% to 1.04%** (Net Vulnerability Shift $-15$, $p = 0.0093$). The extreme overthinking causes severe tool syntax invalidity ($270 \rightarrow 323$), disproving the naive assumption that longer reasoning automatically yields higher attack compliance.
3. **Amplification of the Engagement Requirement (S/F Ratio)**:
   - In models where attacks still succeed under NF4 (Nano and Nanbeige), the S/F ratio increases to **$2.20\times$ and $3.01\times$** (exceeding the $1.15\times$ amplification threshold). Under low precision, models require even deeper reasoning engagement relative to failed trials to successfully navigate the tool-calling schema and execute the injection.
4. **Data Stealing Drives Precision Sensitivity**:
   - Quantization variance is concentrated in multi-step Data Stealing (DS): Qwen DS drops sharply from $29.23\%$ (FP16) to $19.85\%$ (NF4), while Direct Harm remains comparatively stable ($7.65\% \rightarrow 6.27\%$). Similarly, Nano's vulnerability surge under NF4 is driven primarily by DS ($4.78\% \rightarrow 8.82\%$).
